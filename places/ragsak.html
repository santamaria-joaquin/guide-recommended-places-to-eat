🍽️ Ragsak

Ragsak is the perfect place to eat delicious food with your favorite people. The interior is clean, modern, and welcoming simple but very stylish. The food is amazing, servings are generous and everything tastes fresh and well-prepared. It’s the kind of place where you feel comfortable staying for hours, laughing, and enjoying good company. The vibe is great and the service is warm, and the prices are also reasonable too. If you’re looking for great food, cozy space Ragsak is definitely worth visiting!

Detail Information
Address 1276 Maceda St. and the España Branch at 871 Craig Street corner España Boulevard
Opening Hours Mon-Sun: 10am - 2am
Price Range ₱300 - 800₱ per food/drinks
Best Time to Visit Lunch or Early Dinner - Less Crowded
