☕ Cafe Uno

Cafe Uno is the perfect spot to hang out with friends while enjoying great milk tea and coffee. The vibe is super relaxing clean, modern, and peaceful. It’s not just about the drinks; the whole atmosphere makes you want to stay longer. Whether you’re catching up with friends, studying, or just taking a break, Cafe Uno gives you that calm and welcoming feel. Their milk tea is rich and creamy, and the coffee smells amazing the moment you walk in. I highly recommend this place if you want a chill time with good company and great drinks!

Detail Information
Address 1241 Navarra Street, Sampaloc, Manila
Opening Hours Mon–Sun: 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM
Price Range ₱100 - 350₱ per drink
Best Time to Visit Afternoons — calmest and best for hanging out
Must-Try Signature Milk Tea, Iced Coffee
