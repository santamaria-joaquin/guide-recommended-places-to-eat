☕ Kapetina

Kapetina is the perfect mix of friendly vibes and aesthetic atmosphere. The outdoor seating with fairy lights makes it super cozy and relaxing. It’s not just about great coffee, it’s the whole experience. The staff are warm and welcoming, the place feels like a little hidden gem, and every corner is perfect for photos. Since, you’re hanging out with friends, studying, or just enjoying a quiet afternoon, Kapetina gives you that calm and the happy feeling. Genuinely good people a must-visit!
